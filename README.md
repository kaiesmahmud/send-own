# SendOwn — Own Your Email Sending. Stop Renting It.

**Self-hosted SMTP sending, bulk campaigns, open/click tracking, and multi-account rotation — without a per-contact subscription meter.**

> Every other email platform charges you monthly *by how many contacts you have* — whether you send one campaign or twenty. SendOwn flips that: plug in your own email accounts (business domain, Gmail, or Amazon SES), and you own the whole sending pipeline. Pay once. Send from what you already have.

---

## The problem SendOwn solves

Mailchimp, SendGrid, and the rest bill you a growing monthly subscription tied to your list size. A 5,000-contact list runs **$75–100/month, forever**, even in a month you send nothing. For a huge number of businesses — clinics, shops, agencies, SaaS startups, course creators — that meter is pure waste.

SendOwn is the alternative: a dashboard + server you (or I) deploy on your own infrastructure, wired to **your** SMTP accounts. No subscription. No per-contact tax. You own it.

---

## What it does

- **Bring your own email** — business domain SMTP, Gmail (app password), or Amazon SES. Multiple accounts, all in one dashboard.
- **Multi-account rotation** — got three Gmails? Load all three into a campaign. SendOwn sends through the first until it hits its daily limit, then rotates to the next — so you can push a big list across several free accounts automatically. If a provider throttles an account, SendOwn cools it down and keeps going on the others.
- **OTP / verification sending** — drop-in transactional email for signups, password resets, and 2FA.
- **Bulk campaigns** — import contacts by CSV, merge personalization fields, send, and export results as a sheet.
- **Real email editor** — drag-and-drop builder, fixed templates, or paste your own HTML design.
- **Open, click & bounce tracking** — see who opened, who clicked, and what failed, per recipient.
- **Schedule** — set a campaign to go out at a future date and time.
- **Re-engage** — resend to just the people who opened (or didn't) with a new message, in a couple of clicks.
- **Encrypted credentials** — your SMTP secrets are encrypted at rest, never stored in plaintext.
